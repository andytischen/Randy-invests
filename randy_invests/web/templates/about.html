The face of the desk

Meet Randy.

Randy is a middle-class member of the investment community — the person who stays after a PTA meeting to compare 529s, who keeps a watchlist on a yellow pad, and who would rather understand a fee than chase a hot tip. He is not a hedge-fund character and not a luxury influencer. He is the neighbor you would actually ask.

Watch the channel Compare tiers

Why he looks like this

Retail investors are not a costume. The Randy Invests icon is cast as an everyday club member on purpose: unstructured cardigan, kitchen-table research, no supercar, no trading-floor swagger. The brand should feel like someone you already sit next to at an investment club — competent, warm, and unwilling to oversell.

What he will not do on camera

  • Promise returns or “lock in” a trade
  • Treat a model label as a crystal ball
  • Skip the hold-period and fee conversation
  • Pretend this demo desk is a live offering of securities
